## Description

Pencil is a class planner for Northwestern students.

Search every class for the term, see it on your week, and check the things CAESAR makes hard to find: open seats, enrollment requirements, class notes and CTEC ratings. Plan a schedule, see every way your classes fit without overlapping, and add sections to your CAESAR shopping cart without leaving the app.

- Search by course, title or topic
- Your planned, in-cart and enrolled classes as one week
- Live seats and enrollment details from CAESAR
- CTEC ratings, hours and comments for the classes you can view
- Every non-overlapping combination of your planned classes
- Your registration dates
- The walk between your classes on a campus map
- Optional sync between your phone and computer, locked so only your devices can read it

Your data stays on your device. You sign in on Northwestern's own pages, Pencil never sees your password, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Pencil is open source.

Pencil is made by students and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Northwestern University. Live features need campus Wi-Fi or the Northwestern VPN, as CAESAR does.

## Notes for App Review (App Store)

Pencil shows data from Northwestern University's student system (CAESAR). Signing in requires a Northwestern NetID with two-step verification, so we cannot provide a working demo account. Without signing in, the reviewer can use Search, open any course, plan classes on Schedule, view combinations and the Walk map, and turn on Sync from You. The sign-in button opens Northwestern's own sign-in page in a web view; the app does not collect or transmit credentials.

## Answers the store forms will ask for

- **Data collected (both stores):** none. Nothing is collected by the developer; optional sync stores an encrypted copy the developer cannot read.
- **Encryption (App Store export question):** the app uses standard encryption only (HTTPS, and XChaCha20-Poly1305 to lock synced plans).
- **Account required:** no. Sign-in is optional and is Northwestern's, not ours.
- **Ads:** none. **Target audience (Play):** 18 and over.
